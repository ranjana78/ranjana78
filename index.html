About

Turning scarce medical data
into reliable AI.

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Computational and Data Science, Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore. My work sits at the intersection of deep learning and clinical imaging, where labelled data is rare, expensive and spread across institutions.

During my PhD at IIT Ropar, I developed a family of enhanced prototypical networks and sampling strategies for few-shot medical image classification, published at IEEE ISBI and in leading journals. Today I work on federated learning across hospitals and radiomics-guided planning for epilepsy surgery.

Before IISc, I served as an Assistant Professor in Computer Science & Engineering at NIIT University, Neemrana.

Current Research

What I'm working on.

Three active projects spanning privacy-preserving learning, neuro-imaging and women's health.

Federated Learning · IISc

Federated Multi-Prototype Learning for Multi-Centric Medical Image Analysis

Developing FedProIn+, an adaptive, permutation-robust federated framework for heterogeneous medical image classification across hospitals.

Radiomics · Neuro-imaging

Radiomics-Guided Localization of Epilepsy Surgery Targets

A radiomics pipeline that predicts optimal brain resection regions from pre-surgical MRI to support epilepsy surgery planning.

Collaboration · City St George's, London

EECC: Hierarchical Fine-Tuning for Endometrial Cancer Classification

With Dr. Sujit Biswas (University of London), an efficient framework for the early detection of endometrial cancer in women.
